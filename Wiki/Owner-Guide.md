# 👑 Owner Persona Guide

As the **Master / Owner**, you set daily routine expectations, reward good behavior, log calendar status, and provide positive reinforcement.

---

## 1. Daily Tasks & Routines

The **Daily Tasks** section allows you to create daily routine checklist items for your partner.

* **Adding a Task:** Navigate to the Home screen, click **+ Add Task**, enter the task title (e.g., *"Drink 2L Fresh Water 💧"*), and set the XP reward (default: 25 XP).
* **3-Dots Menu:** Tap the 3-dots icon on any task to:
  - **Override Task:** Force mark a task as completed or incomplete.
  - **Delete Task:** Remove a task permanently.
* **Auto Day-End Reset:** All daily task checkmarks automatically reset at midnight every day so your Pet starts each day with a fresh checklist!

---

## 2. Calendar Status Logging

Track historic discipline and routine quality on the interactive calendar:

* 🟢 **Green Day:** All tasks completed & exemplary behavior. Earns full reward points!
* 🟡 **Yellow Day:** Partial task completion or minor slip-ups.
* 🔴 **Red Day:** Missed routines or discipline reset.
* **Auto-Evaluation:** Completing all tasks automatically updates today's calendar status to **Green 🟢**.

---

## 3. Reward Store & Redemption Approvals

Under the **Rewards Hub** (`rewards` tab):

* **Create Reward Items:** Add custom rewards to the store with a point cost (e.g., *"30 Min Back Massage 💆" - 100 pts*).
* **Review Proposals:** When your Pet submits a new reward idea, approve it to add it to the active store catalog or reject it.
* **Process Redemptions:** When your Pet redeems a reward, mark it as **Fulfilled** once delivered.

---

## 4. Praise Transmitter & Instant Nudges

Deliver instant encouragement and quick check-in reminders:

* **Praise Cards:** Send a heart-stamped praise note with audio-visual confetti effects that pop up on your Pet's screen.
* **Instant Nudges:** Tap quick 1-click presets:
  - *Drink Water 💧*
  - *Time to Stretch 🐾*
  - *Bedtime Check-in 🛌*
  - *Head Pats 💖*
