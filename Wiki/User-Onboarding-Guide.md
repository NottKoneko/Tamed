# 🚀 User Onboarding & Account Pairing Guide

Welcome to Tamed! This guide walks you through signing up, selecting your role, and linking accounts with your partner.

---

## 1. Creating Your Account & Signing In

1. Open **[tamed.pages.dev](https://tamed.pages.dev/)** in your desktop or mobile browser.
2. If Supabase is configured:
   - Click **Sign Up** to create an account with your Email & Password.
   - Or click **Sign In** if you already have an active account.
3. If using **Demo Mode**:
   - You can proceed directly without an email address to test out all features locally!

---

## 2. Setting Up Your Persona Profile

When you log in for the first time, you will be prompted to complete onboarding:

### Select Your Role:
* **👑 Master / Owner:** You manage daily behavior tasks, log day quality (Green/Yellow/Red), approve reward redemptions, and send praise notes.
* **🦊 Pet / Submissive:** You complete daily checklists to earn XP, gain levels, earn species currency (Bones 🦴, Fish 🐟, Berries 🫐, Stars ✨), and redeem custom rewards.

### Customization Options:
* **Username:** The display name your partner sees.
* **Species Persona (Pet):** Choose from Puppy 🐶, Kitty 🐱, Fox 🦊, or Custom ✨.
* **Praise Terms:** Custom endearing names used by your partner (e.g., *"Good girl!"*, *"Good boy!"*).
* **Theme Colors:** Pick custom primary and accent theme colors.

---

## 3. Account Pairing via 6-Digit Code

To sync routines, calendars, and points in real-time, both partners must be paired:

```
┌─────────────────────────┐               ┌─────────────────────────┐
│     👑 OWNER ACCOUNT    │               │      🦊 PET ACCOUNT     │
│                         │               │                         │
│ 1. View 6-Digit Code    ├──────────────►│ 2. Enter Pair Code      │
│    (e.g., "789123")     │  Share Code   │    in Onboarding        │
└────────────┬────────────┘               └────────────┬────────────┘
             │                                         │
             └───────────────────┬─────────────────────┘
                                 │
                     ┌───────────▼───────────┐
                     │   ✨ ACCOUNTS PAIRED   │
                     │  Real-Time Sync Active │
                     └───────────────────────┘
```

1. **Owner:** Copy your unique 6-digit **Pair Code** shown on your screen.
2. **Pet:** Paste or enter your Owner's 6-digit Pair Code into the onboarding form.
3. Click **Link Accounts**. Your dashboards will immediately sync in real-time!

> [!TIP]
> You can disconnect or repair accounts at any time under **Settings > Pair Configuration**.
